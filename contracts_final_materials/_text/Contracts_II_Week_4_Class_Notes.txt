Does one party’s breach excuse a non-breaching party’s due to perform?
Partial breach-does not excuse non-breaching party from their duty.
Total breach- can excuse breaching party from their duty.
Distinction between total and material breach- material Breach is serious deviation from performance obligation under terms of agreement. However, this deviation can be cured. 
Total breach- uncured total breach.
Progression from material breach to total breach—curable to uncurable and excusing the non-breaching party’s duty under the terms of the agreement.
Clear manifestation not to perform the contract-anticipatory repudiation.
Doubtful/ambiguous not to perform, not anticipatory repudiation
Change position or seek alternative buying/selling option. But a repudiation is retracted so long as the non-repudiating party tells the repudiating party that they treat the repudiation as final or they’ve changed positions.

# Midterm E.Q. benchmarks (user sent 11/24). Every damages question must match these.

## Format features
- **Length:** 250-300 words of facts, written as one dense paragraph.
- **Structure:** each fact pattern drives 1-4 numbered sub-questions (Loss in Value / Other Loss / Cost Avoided / Loss Avoided / General Measure). Sub-question numbering continues across patterns (1-14).
- **Distractors:** each pattern has 2-4 traps:
  - a cost the plaintiff would have paid anyway (gym, insurance renewal)
  - an unforeseeable collateral profit (the video sales)
  - attorney's fees when the contract says nothing about them
  - a speculative renewal
  - a "superior replacement" argument
  - a market price next to the price actually paid
  - an irrelevant contract-cost figure
- **Wording:** "reasonable," "customary," "equivalent quality," and "materially lower responsibility and professional status" each signal the rule to apply.
- **Answer format:** on the final, each component becomes a multiple-choice question with figure choices.
